this package does not grant new rights to the underlying images, captions, articles, identities,
logos, or other third-party content.

By accessing the packaged media, users agree to:

1. use the dataset for legitimate research, education, evaluation, and reproducibility purposes;
2. review and comply with the licenses and terms of every relevant source dataset;
3. avoid using the data to harass, profile, identify, or make consequential decisions about people;
4. avoid presenting benchmark labels as definitive statements about depicted individuals;
5. preserve source attribution and the OctantFake citation in redistributed derived metadata;
6. not redistribute underlying media when the applicable source terms do not permit it; and
7. report removal or rights concerns through the dataset repository's contact mechanism.

underlying source licenses and do not limit rights already granted by those licenses. Repository
